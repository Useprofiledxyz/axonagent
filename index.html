// FAQ

    
Questions

    
What is AXON, in one line?
An AI agent that writes working web apps from a description. You chat, it builds, you download the result. Holding the AXON token unlocks it.

    
Who can use the studio?
Wallets on Robinhood Chain (MetaMask, Rabby or any EVM wallet) that hold at least the required amount of the token. Connect, sign a free message (no transaction), and the studio unlocks for 12 hours.

    
What can Axon build?
Single-page web apps: dashboards, games, landing pages, tools, calculators, trackers. Everything runs in the browser in one HTML file.

    
Who owns what Axon makes?
You do. Download the file and use it however you like.

    
Is there a limit?
Yes, a number of builds per wallet per day, shown in the studio. It keeps the agent fast for every holder.

    
Where are my projects saved?
In your browser on this device. Download anything you want to keep for good.

    
Does signing in cost anything?
No. Signing the message is free and does not send a transaction. Axon never asks for your seed phrase.
